The Career

From Jos to the Inner Bar

A single unbroken line of practice, study and scholarship.

1999Bachelor of Laws, University of Jos

The foundation of a career that would turn toward the commercial and the technological.

2001Called to the Nigerian Bar, Abuja

Barrister at Law, Nigerian Law School.

2005M.Sc. Financial Management, Middlesex University Business School, London

Financial training that underpins the project finance, capital markets and insolvency practice.

2007Punuka Attorneys and Solicitors, Lagos

Early years under the distinguished leadership of Dr. Anthony Idigbe, SAN. Led the team behind Nigeria's first class action on electronic transactions, brought against all Nigerian commercial banks and the Central Bank of Nigeria over unauthorised ATM withdrawals.

2011Co-founds Accendolaw

Barristers and Solicitors, growing into a commercial practice in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos.

2017Notary Public of the Federal Republic of Nigeria

Appointed to the office of Notary Public.

2023Senior Advocate of Nigeria

Conferred with the highest distinction in the Nigerian legal profession, in recognition of extraordinary contributions to the development of law in Nigeria. The rank is the equivalent of King's Counsel in the United Kingdom.

2024Accendolaw Partners LP

The practice elevated to a limited partnership. He serves as Head of Dispute Resolution.

2025Chairman, Editorial Board, NBA Lagos Branch

Leading the Branch's editorial work, and building the scholarship that will carry into the forthcoming textbook.

A Forthcoming Textbook The Law of Cyberspace and Decentralised Finance Tochukwu J. Onyiuke, SAN
In Preparation · Publication Targeted January

A global book for a borderless subject

Where does the law end and the blockchain begin? Drawing on eleven articles in the C.T.L.R. and on frontline practice in technology disputes, this textbook maps the legal architecture of cyberspace and decentralised finance for practitioners, regulators and the academy.

Its subject is global, and its readership is global. This portal is built to serve that readership: the authoritative index of the author's scholarship, pointing every reader to the publisher who sells the work.
